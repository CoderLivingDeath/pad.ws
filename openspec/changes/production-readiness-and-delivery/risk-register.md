# Risk register — production readiness and delivery

This register records risks and problems **with the decisions** in this change. Every accepted risk has an owner, mitigation and a link to the governing spec requirement. The normative requirements live in the change specs; this file is the operational risk view.

**Severity:** Critical / High / Medium / Low.
**Status:** accepted / mitigated / follow-up / open.

## Decision log

| Decision | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offsite backup | Not in this change | Operator chose “nowhere for now”; local retention and integrity are implemented, offsite is a follow-up |
| Automated CD | Not in this change | Current scope is “ordinary tests and lints”; deployment/rollback remain explicit operator commands |
| Rate limiting | No custom Caddy build now | Standard Caddy lacks `rate_limit`; Keycloak brute-force plus app-level limits are follow-up |

## Review rule

A blocking risk without an entry here fails readiness review. When a risk is closed, change its status to `mitigated`/`closed` and link the evidence (test, drill record or deployment record). The register is reviewed together with `docs/testing.md` and the Use Case matrix.
